FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What services does Lumina Studio offer?

Lumina Studio provides interior design, space planning, home styling, furniture selection, renovation guidance, and complete interior transformation services for residential spaces.

Do you work with different design styles?

Yes. We tailor every project to reflect your personal style, whether you prefer modern luxury, contemporary minimalism, timeless elegance, or a more curated aesthetic.

Can Lumina Studio manage a project from start to finish?

Absolutely. We handle every stage of the design process, from concept development and material selection to styling and final execution.

Do you offer consultations before starting a project?

Yes. We begin with a consultation to understand your vision, lifestyle, preferences, and project goals before creating a tailored design approach.

How long does an interior project usually take?

Project timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of the space, but we always aim to deliver exceptional results within a well-structured timeframe.
